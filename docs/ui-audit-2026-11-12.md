# The UI audit of 12 November 2026

What was checked: the desktop app's interface (`client/ui`), every file read, then run in Chromium on the mock backend (`js/mock.js`). Pier dark and Pier light at 1280 by 800 and 760 by 600, and once with reduced motion; the setup, trust, workspace, palette, settings, menus and dialogs clicked or keyed through; contrast computed for the 24 built-in themes. The look it keeps and the motif it adds: [Design](design.md).

Severity: **high**, fails WCAG 2.2 AA or makes something unusable; **medium**, works, but badly for some; **low**, polish.

## Kept, and why

- **The caption buttons (minimise, maximise, close) get the hand too.** Pier draws them, and they are buttons like the others. Windows' own caption buttons keep the arrow: it is one rule to take out if that matters more.
- **One motif, in two places.** No dithered pictures, loaders or backgrounds: see [Design](design.md).

## Seen, not changed

- **Solarized's own text.** Its `fg` reads 4.44:1 on `--surface` (dark), and on an added diff line 3.72:1 (dark) and 4.18:1 (light). `completeTheme` derives dim, the edges and the accent and leaves a theme's own colours alone; it could nudge `fg` as well.
- **A narrow Conversations panel** (a 760 px window) wraps a lone "·" onto a line of its own in the facts of a project.
- **Dialogs over a light theme** dim the page with the same black at 55% as over a dark one: heavy on white.
- **Not run here:** the app itself in Tauri (WebView2 on Windows, WebKit on macOS and Linux), as every check ran in Chromium; the give-a-folder dialog in the browser; `cargo test`, with no Rust toolchain in the audit's sandbox (no Rust changed).

## How it was checked

- `node --test client/test/dither.test.js client/test/themes.test.js`: 37 tests, all pass.
- `python3 -B pier-picker-unit.py`: 82 tests, OK.
- Chromium with Playwright, on `client/ui/index.html` and the mock backend, five runs (Pier dark and light at 1280 by 800 and 760 by 600, Pier dark with reduced motion): no console error in any, the checks in the table above, and a screenshot of every state, each looked at.
- Contrast before and after, all 24 themes, with the `contrast` of `themes.js`.

The two scripts behind the last two points came with the audit, outside the repository, as the browser one needs Playwright: `qa/after.mjs` and `qa/contrast.mjs`.
