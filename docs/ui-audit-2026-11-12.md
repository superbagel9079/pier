# The UI audit of 12 November 2026

What was checked: the desktop app's interface (`client/ui`), every file read, then run in Chromium on the mock backend (`js/mock.js`). Pier dark and Pier light at 1280 by 800 and 760 by 600, and once with reduced motion; the setup, trust, workspace, palette, settings, menus and dialogs clicked or keyed through; contrast computed for the 24 built-in themes. The look it keeps and the motif it adds: [Design](design.md).

Severity: **high**, fails WCAG 2.2 AA or makes something unusable; **medium**, works, but badly for some; **low**, polish.

## Kept, and why

- **The caption buttons (minimise, maximise, close) get the hand too.** Pier draws them, and they are buttons like the others. Windows' own caption buttons keep the arrow: it is one rule to take out if that matters more.
- **One motif, in two places.** No dithered pictures, loaders or backgrounds: see [Design](design.md).

## A second pass, the next day

What the first pass had left, and what a second audit found, all fixed:

- **Errors were not read out.** `showError` and the dialogs' error lines are `role="alert"`; the connection's status line is `aria-live="polite"` (WCAG 4.1.3).
- **ERROR log lines were in the theme's red**, under 4.5:1 in 16 of the 24 themes (2.37:1 in Gruvbox dark). They are marked as `.error` is: a bar and bold text.
- **Pier's text could not be enlarged** (WCAG 1.4.4). `zoomHotkeysEnabled`: Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-; macOS and Linux need `core:webview:allow-set-webview-zoom`.
- **Solarized's own text** read 3.72:1 on an added diff line. `completeTheme` now nudges a theme's `fg` too, until it reads 4.5:1 on its surfaces and the diff's tints.
- **The splitters** are drawn 4 px wide and grabbed 12 px wide; **icon buttons** are at least 24 px wide (WCAG 2.5.8).
- **A lone "·"** no longer wraps onto a line of its own: the dot stays with the fact before it.
- **Dialogs over a light theme**: the page steps back into the theme's own background, not a black veil.
- **A double click on Hand off** set two keyboard traps and released one: every Escape stopped short of its target, the terminal included. It opens once.

Left: a pointer can set a side's width only by dragging (WCAG 2.5.7); the keyboard, a double click and Collapse are the other ways. Not run here: the app itself in Tauri, as every check ran in Chromium.
